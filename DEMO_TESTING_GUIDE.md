## 🌟 **Testing the Complete Flow**

### **Step 1: Visit Sign-Up Page**
**URL**: `http://localhost:3001/auth/signup`

### **Step 2: Quick Sign-Up Options**

#### **Option A: One-Click Demo (Fastest)**
1. Click **"🚀 Demo Data"** button
2. Click **"Create account"**
3. Get instant access to dashboard

#### **Option B: Random Email**
1. Click **"🎲 Random Email"** button
2. Auto-generates: `user123@example.com` + name
3. Click **"Create account"**

#### **Option C: Custom Email**
1. Enter any email: `test@anything.com`, `hello@world`, `user123`
2. Enter any name (optional)
3. Password is optional
4. Click **"Create account"**

#### **Option D: Keyboard Shortcut**
1. Fill any fields
2. Press **Ctrl+Enter** to submit quickly
